# AquaSentinel: 3-Minute Demo Script

## 1. The Problem (30s)
"Clean water is a human right, but every year millions suffer from preventable waterborne diseases. The problem isn't just lack of water; it's a lack of *predictive intelligence*. By the time an outbreak is reported, it's already too late. We need to shift from reactive to proactive."

## 2. The Solution (30s)
"Meet AquaSentinel. We've built an intelligent platform that predicts outbreaks 7 days before they happen. We fuse live IoT water quality data, rainfall forecasts, and community sanitation reports into a single AI engine."

## 3. Live Demo (90s)
* **Overview Page**: "Our dashboard gives policymakers an instant view of city-wide risks. Notice the beautiful, modern interface."
* **Live Monitoring**: "Here we're ingesting live sensor data. Watch as an anomaly is flagged in Ward 4—turbidity has spiked." (Show gauge turning red).
* **Outbreak Prediction**: "Our Random Forest model processes this, along with recent rainfall, and predicts an 85% risk of a disease surge in Ward 7 next week. It even explains *why* (show feature importance)."
* **Action & Export**: "We don't just predict; we act. The system recommends dispatching medical camps and auto-generates a PDF report for officials with one click."

## 4. Impact & Scalability (30s)
"AquaSentinel directly addresses SDG 6. It's scalable—you can plug in real IoT sensors tomorrow, or upload CSVs if you're offline. We're ready to protect communities."
